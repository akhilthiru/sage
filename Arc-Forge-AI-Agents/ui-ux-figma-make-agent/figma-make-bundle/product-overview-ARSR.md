# Product Overview — ARSR

---

## What Is ARSR?

ARSR is a web-based supply chain intelligence platform built for the Australian automotive industry. It enables manufacturers and their supply chain partners to manage Bill of Materials (BOM) data, monitor material compliance against IMDS standards, and gain visibility into supply chain risk across their parts network.

---

## Domain Context

The automotive supply chain involves complex, multi-tier supplier networks where material composition data must be accurately tracked for regulatory compliance (IMDS, REACH, EU ELV Directive). Managing this data — from initial BOM upload through to risk analysis — is the core purpose of ARSR.

Key domain terms:

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **BOM** | Bill of Materials — the structured list of all parts, materials, and quantities in a vehicle or assembly |
| **IMDS** | International Material Data System — the automotive industry standard for exchanging material data between suppliers and OEMs |
| **Data Coverage** | The percentage of BOM parts for which complete IMDS data is available |
| **Production Plan** | A forecast linking a BOM to planned production volumes |
| **Risk Insights** | Analysis of supply chain exposure — supplier concentration, substance compliance, geographic risk |

---

## Users

ARSR is designed for supply chain professionals within Australian automotive organisations:

| Role | Primary responsibility |
|------|----------------------|
| Risk Manager | Monitor supply chain exposure and prioritise remediation |
| Supply Chain Analyst | Upload and manage BOMs, resolve data coverage gaps |
| Compliance Officer | Verify substance reporting completeness |
| Production Planner | Link BOMs to production forecasts |

---

## Platform Context

- **Deployment:** Desktop web application, 1440px viewport
- **Market:** Australian automotive OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers
- **Language:** English (Australian spelling and conventions)
- **Regulatory alignment:** IMDS, REACH, EU ELV Directive, Australian Privacy Act 1988
